Zone 13

There are 32 players on the map and they fight each other too. The last one alive wins. You can enter buildings through their doors, and there is loot inside. The blue zone slowly shrinks, and if you stay outside it your health drops.

Mobile: Move with the left joystick and drag on the screen to look around. The aim button shoots, the circular arrow reloads, tap the weapon box at the top-right to switch guns, the bomb button throws a bomb, the vehicle button gets you in or out of a vehicle, the running man is sprint (tap once to turn on, again to turn off), the up arrow jumps, the sitting man crouches and the lying man goes prone. Red dots on the top-left minimap are players. Use the 🎛 button next to the minimap to customize your HUD.

PC: WASD to move, mouse to look, click or F to shoot, R reload, 1-8 or Q to switch guns, G bomb, E vehicle, Shift sprint, Space jump, C crouch, Z prone (you can move while prone), H to customize your HUD.

Pick up the green (health), blue (armor), yellow (ammo) and orange (bomb) boxes lying around. Shoot the red barrels to cause an explosion.
